\section{Nixie tube characteristics}
\label{sec:tubes}

The design uses six cold-cathode gas-discharge indicator tubes. Four tubes show
the hours and minutes. Two tubes make the colon. \Cref{tab:tube-designators}
defines their designators.

Both parts have scanned Soviet factory datasheets. The tables copy the values
from these scans. They include the Russian parameter name when the English
meaning is not clear. The datasheets do not always give minimum, typical, and
maximum values. Some values are one-sided limits. The anode-supply design uses
these limits conservatively.

\paragraph{Data not specified.} Neither source specifies total anode current,
interelectrode capacitance, anode-to-cathode capacitance, a ballast resistance,
or a target anode current in the \SIrange{2.5}{3.5}{\milli\ampere} range.

\paragraph{Open item: anode supply voltage.} The datasheet specifies a minimum
anode supply of \SI{200}{\volt} and recommends \SI{250}{\volt}. The derived
anode-rail range is \SIrange{164.8}{191.5}{\volt}
(\cref{sec:hv-converter}). Thus, the design does not meet the specified minimum.

The factory value can apply to unfiltered half-wave AC (inferred). In this
mode, the supply stays near zero during approximately half of each mains cycle.
The peak voltage must then exceed the \SI{145}{\volt} typical maintaining
voltage. This condition gives sufficient average current and reliable ignition.
A regulated DC supply does not have this waveform. The datasheet does not
explain the difference.

\Cref{sec:hv-converter} keeps this item open. During bench adjustment, increase
the output until all installed digits ignite reliably. Then set the output
approximately \SI{10}{\volt} above this value.

\subsection{INS-1 (colon tubes, NX5 to NX6)}
\label{sec:ins1}

Source: \texttt{INS-1.pdf}, Soviet factory datasheet ODO.334.095 TU. Count the
two leads from the indicator dot. Lead 1 is the anode, which is the small
cylinder. Lead 2 is the cathode, which is the large cylinder. The tube has no

\paragraph{Conditioning after storage.} After long storage, operate the tube at
its nominal discharge current for one to two minutes. The datasheet refers to
GOST 11163-84 and OST 11~339.003-75. The IN-12B has a similar instruction
(\cref{sec:in12b}).

\paragraph{Data not specified.} The datasheet gives no minimum maintaining
voltage. It gives no operating-current range around the
\SI{0.5}{\milli\ampere} brightness value. It also gives no maximum switching
frequency, interelectrode capacitance, ballast value, or envelope dimensions.

\Cref{sec:hv-load-colon} sizes the ballast from the
\SI{0.5}{\milli\ampere} value. It also gives the current range caused by output
adjustment. A tube with a maintaining voltage below \SI{55}{\volt} draws more
current through a fixed ballast. The datasheet gives no lower limit for this
calculation.
