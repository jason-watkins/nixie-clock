\section{170~V converter: driving component characteristics}
\label{sec:hv-components}

The 170~V anode supply uses a current-mode flyback converter. Its main parts
are a TI LM51561H controller and a Coilcraft DA2032-AL transformer.
\Cref{sec:hv-converter} uses these characteristics and assigns the designators.

\paragraph{Current-limit slope compensation.} The \SI{100}{\milli\volt}
current-limit threshold is a fixed DC level at the CS pin. The peak-current
equation subtracts slope compensation only when optional resistor \(R_{SL}\) is
installed. TI recommends that the designer omit this resistor for an accurate
limit across \(V_{IN}\). A separate internal slope ramp enters only the PWM/COMP
comparator. It prevents subharmonic oscillation. It does not enter the
current-limit comparator. Thus, \cref{sec:hv-cs} uses the fixed
\SI{100}{\milli\volt} threshold.

\paragraph{Soft-start formula.} The datasheet gives soft-start equations only
for boost and SEPIC converters. Its nonisolated flyback example connects
FB, SS, and COMP as in the boost circuit. However, the boost equation assumes
an input-to-output conduction path. A flyback transformer has no such path.
Thus, \cref{sec:hv-ss} derives the time from the SS charging current. The
LM51561H soft start must continue until the output reaches regulation.
Otherwise, hiccup protection operates at each start.

\paragraph{EN and UVLO comparators.} Pin 14 has separate EN and UVLO
comparators. The approximately \SI{0.5}{\volt} EN threshold controls shutdown
and standby. The approximately \SI{1.5}{\volt} rising and
\SI{1.45}{\volt} falling UVLO thresholds control standby and operation. An
input divider sets the UVLO voltage. Its design uses the UVLO threshold and
hysteresis current.

\paragraph{Maximum duty cycle.} The frequency-dependent \(D_{MAX}\) is
approximately \SIrange{91}{92}{\percent} near \SI{150}{\kilo\hertz}
(inferred). This limit constrains the transformer turns ratio at minimum input
voltage. \Cref{sec:hv-dcm} checks the design against this limit.

The secondary is on pins 1 and 12. The primary windings are on pins 3 to 10.
The polarity dot identifies pin 1. The datasheet gives no operating-frequency
range. It characterizes inductance and interwinding capacitance at
\SI{100}{\kilo\hertz}. Thus, operation at \SI{150}{\kilo\hertz} is an
extrapolation (inferred).

The datasheet gives no saturation current. It specifies a minimum inductance
of \SI{9.0}{\micro\henry} at \SI{3.0}{\ampere}. This analysis uses
\SI{3.0}{\ampere} as the effective peak-current rating (inferred). The
isolation ratings assume an isolated circuit. This board connects the
secondary return to primary ground. This external connection removes circuit
isolation. The transformer windings remain galvanically separate. The
datasheet does not prohibit this connection.
