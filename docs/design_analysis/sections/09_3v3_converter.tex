The supply powers the ESP32 module and two power-good pull-ups.
\des{R}{3V3PG} also pulls up the MCU reset input (\cref{sec:esp32-reset}). Wi-Fi
transmit bursts cause the main load transients. The input is the OR of
$V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ and the serial-header supply. The header can supply nominal
\SI{3.3}{\volt} or \SI{5}{\volt}. Because the input can be below the output,
the supply uses a buck-boost converter.

\subsection{Load budget}\label{sec:3v3-load}

The MCU module is the main load. Its maximum specified sustained current is
\SI{355}{\milli\ampere} during Wi-Fi transmission. Espressif requires an
external supply capacity of at least \SI{0.5}{\ampere}
(\cref{sec:esp32}).

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{BCD drive lines.} Sixteen GPIOs drive the four K155ID1s' BCD inputs. A TTL
input driven high draws $I_{IH} \leq \SI{40}{\micro\ampere}$ (A input) or
\SI{80}{\micro\ampere} (B/C/D inputs) from the driving pin (\cref{sec:esp32-bcd}); a
line driven low \emph{sinks} current sourced from the drivers' 5~V rail and adds nothing
to VDD33. Worst case, all sixteen lines high:
\begin{align*}
  I_{\mathrm{BCD}} &= 4 \times (40 + 3\times80)\,\si{\micro\ampere}
    = \SI{1.12}{\milli\ampere},
\end{align*}
\item \emph{Pull-ups.} \des{R}{HVPG} (the flyback's power-good) and \des{R}{3V3PG} (this
converter's, \cref{sec:3v3-house}) each contribute at most
\SI{33}{\micro\ampere}, and only during power-not-good windows.
\Cref{sec:esp32-pgood,sec:esp32-reset} quantify them.
\item \emph{Serial buffers.} The two logic buffers of \cref{sec:esp32-uart} draw
\SI{4}{\micro\ampere} each at most, plus their idle-state pull-ups at
\SI{330}{\micro\ampere} each while a line is held low.
\item \emph{Port-controller interfaces.} The three \SI{10}{\kilo\ohm} HPI
pull-ups draw at most \SI{0.99}{\milli\ampere}. The USB-switch
\SI{10}{\kilo\ohm} pull-up draws \SI{0.33}{\milli\ampere} when the MCU holds
the switch enabled.
\end{itemize}

The design totals are therefore
\begin{align*}
  I_{\mathrm{load,max}} &= 355 + 1.12 + 0.07 + 0.70 + 0.99 + 0.33
    \approx \SI{358}{\milli\ampere} \approx \SI{0.36}{\ampere},\\
  P_{\mathrm{out,max}} &= 3.3 \times 0.358 = \SI{1.18}{\watt}.
\end{align*}

Housekeeping current is less than \SI{3.3}{\milli\ampere}. Therefore, the design
uses the Espressif \SI{0.5}{\ampere} supply-capacity requirement.

\subsection{Operating point}\label{sec:3v3-input}

The converter input is the OR node in \cref{sec:3v3-diodeor}. Its voltage
depends on the available source:
\begin{description}
  \item[Bus-powered, high-voltage contract] \SI{9}{\volt} or \SI{12}{\volt},
    $V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ \SIrange{8.55}{12.6}{\volt} through \des{Q}{3V3BUS}, giving
    \SIrange{8.53}{12.6}{\volt} at the node.
  \item[Bus-powered, \SI{5}{\volt} result] either operating class of
    \cref{sec:pd-classes}, $V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ \SIrange{4.75}{5.5}{\volt}, giving
    \SIrange{4.71}{5.47}{\volt} at the node.
  \item[Header-powered] an externally supplied \SI{3.3}{\volt} or \SI{5}{\volt}
    through \des{Q}{3V3HDR}, giving \SI{3.15}{\volt} at worst (\cref{sec:3v3-diodeor} derives
    both figures).
\end{description}
All bands are inside the \SIrange{2.0}{16}{\volt} operating range. The
\SI{12.6}{\volt} maximum has a factor of 1.59 margin below the
\SI{20}{\volt} absolute maximum. The bus-powered input tracks
$V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ within \SI{25}{\milli\volt}. The header-powered input must
also exceed the \SI{3.0}{\volt} start-up requirement.

Duty cycle at the extremes of each band:
\begin{align*}
  \text{buck: } D &= \frac{V_{OUT}}{V_{IN}}
    &&\SI{12.6}{\volt}: 0.262; \quad \SI{8.53}{\volt}: 0.387; \quad
      \SI{4.71}{\volt}: 0.701, \\
  \text{boost: } D &= \frac{V_{OUT}-V_{IN}}{V_{OUT}}
    &&\SI{3.15}{\volt}: 0.045.
\end{align*}

\paragraph{Minimum buck duty cycle.} The general minimum duty cycle is
\SI{30}{\percent}. It decreases to \SI{20}{\percent} with at least
\SI{40}{\micro\farad} effective output capacitance and
\SIrange{0.7}{1.8}{\micro\henry} effective inductance. At maximum input, the
duty cycle is \SI{26.2}{\percent}. Therefore, the design must meet the
capacitance and inductance conditions for the lower limit.

\subsection{Inductor}\label{sec:3v3-inductor}

The general effective-inductance range is
\SIrange{0.7}{2.8}{\micro\henry}. The duty-cycle condition narrows it to
\SIrange{0.7}{1.8}{\micro\henry}. With the specified $+20/-30\,\%$ effective
tolerance, a nominal \SI{1.5}{\micro\henry} part spans
\SIrange{1.05}{1.8}{\micro\henry}. Therefore,
$\des{L}{3V3} = \SI{1.5}{\micro\henry}$.

\paragraph{Peak current.} Boost and buck operation peak under different
conditions. In boost mode, use the datasheet peak-current expression. The
calculation uses a \SI{3.15}{\volt} input and a \SI{0.5}{\ampere} output:
\begin{align*}
  I_{PEAK} &= \frac{I_{OUT}}{\eta(1-D)} + \frac{V_{IN} D}{2 f L}
    = \frac{0.5}{0.9\times0.955} + \frac{3.15\times0.045}
      {2\times\SI{2.4}{\mega\hertz}\times\SI{1.5}{\micro\henry}}
    = \SI{0.602}{\ampere}.
\end{align*}
In buck mode the peak is the load plus half the ripple, worst at the maximum
input and at the tolerance extremes that maximize ripple (effective inductance
\SI{1.05}{\micro\henry}, oscillator at its \SI{2.1}{\mega\hertz} minimum):
\begin{align*}
  \Delta I_L &= \frac{V_{OUT}(V_{IN}-V_{OUT})}{V_{IN} L f}
    = \frac{3.3\,(12.6-3.3)}{12.6\times\SI{1.05}{\micro\henry}\times\SI{2.1}{\mega\hertz}}
    = \SI{1.10}{\ampere},
  &
  I_{PEAK} &= 0.5 + \frac{1.10}{2} = \SI{1.05}{\ampere}.
\end{align*}
The buck condition gives the larger current. The recommended $20\,\%$ margin
requires $I_{\mathrm{sat}} \geq \SI{1.26}{\ampere}$ in normal operation. The
maximum converter current limit is \SI{4.15}{\ampere}. Therefore, specify
$I_{\mathrm{sat}} \geq \SI{4.2}{\ampere}$ and DCR no more than
\SI{50}{\milli\ohm}.

\paragraph{Right-half-plane zero.} The datasheet requires the boost
right-half-plane zero to exceed \SI{400}{\kilo\hertz}. Minimum input and maximum
load give the lowest frequency:
\begin{align*}
  f_{RHPZ} &= \frac{(1-D)^2 V_{OUT}}{2\pi I_{OUT} L}
    = \frac{0.955^2 \times 3.3}
      {2\pi \times 0.5 \times \SI{1.5}{\micro\henry}} = \SI{638}{\kilo\hertz},
\end{align*}
The result has a factor of 1.6 margin. At the
\SI{0.358}{\ampere} sustained load, the margin factor is 2.2.

\subsection{Output voltage programming}\label{sec:3v3-fb}

The adjustable variant regulates its feedback pin to \SI{800}{\milli\volt}, so
the divider ratio sets the output:
\begin{align*}
  \des{R}{3V3D1} &= \des{R}{3V3D2}\left(\frac{V_{OUT}}{\SI{0.8}{\volt}}-1\right)
    = \des{R}{3V3D2}\times 3.125.
\end{align*}
Feedback leakage and quiescent current set the divider impedance. The maximum
feedback leakage is \SI{100}{\nano\ampere}. It causes an output error of
$I_{lkg}\des{R}{3V3D1}$. Smaller resistors decrease this error but increase
quiescent current. The lower resistor must not exceed \SI{400}{\kilo\ohm}.

$\des{R}{3V3D2} = \SI{39}{\kilo\ohm}$ gives a
\SI{20.5}{\micro\ampere} divider current. The maximum leakage error is
\SI{12}{\milli\volt}. The exact upper resistance is
\SI{121.9}{\kilo\ohm}. The two adjacent standard values give
\begin{align*}
  \des{R}{3V3D1} = \SI{120}{\kilo\ohm} &\rightarrow V_{OUT} = \SI{3.262}{\volt}, &
  \des{R}{3V3D1} = \SI{130}{\kilo\ohm} &\rightarrow V_{OUT} = \SI{3.467}{\volt}.
\end{align*}
Use the lower value. With \SI{130}{\kilo\ohm}, the Power Save Mode offset can
raise the output to \SI{3.57}{\volt}. This value is too close to the
\SI{3.6}{\volt} MCU absolute maximum. Therefore, specify
$\des{R}{3V3D1} = \SI{120}{\kilo\ohm}$, $\des{R}{3V3D2} = \SI{39}{\kilo\ohm}$, both 1\,\% and from
one resistor series so their temperature coefficients track and the ratio does
not drift; $V_{OUT} = \SI{3.262}{\volt}$ programmed.

\subsection{Output capacitance, transient response, and rail accuracy}\label{sec:3v3-cout}

Three requirements set the effective output capacitance:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \emph{Filter ratio.} The adjustable variant requires effective
    capacitance in \si{\micro\farad} at least ten times the effective inductance
    in \si{\micro\henry} (\cref{sec:tps63070}): at the \SI{1.8}{\micro\henry}
    upper extreme, $\geq\SI{18}{\micro\farad}$.
  \item \emph{Minimum duty relaxation.} \Cref{sec:3v3-input} requires
    $\geq\SI{40}{\micro\farad}$ effective, the binding one of the three.
  \item \emph{Recommended range.} \SIrange{15}{470}{\micro\farad} total at the
    output pin, which bounds the specification from above.
\end{itemize}
The analysis assumes that output capacitors retain at least $70\,\%$ of nominal
capacitance at \SI{3.3}{\volt} (inferred). Verify this value for the selected
parts. At this retention, \SI{57}{\micro\farad} nominal gives
\SI{40}{\micro\farad} effective.

The datasheet's own filter table pairs a \SI{1.5}{\micro\henry} inductor with
\SI{47}{\micro\farad}, \SI{68}{\micro\farad}, or \SI{100}{\micro\farad} of
nominal output capacitance, and separates bulk capacitance from a small-body
part at the output pin (\cref{sec:tps63070}). Specification:
\des{C}{3V3O3} $= \SI{47}{\micro\farad}$ and \des{C}{3V3O2} $= \SI{22}{\micro\farad}$ as bulk,
\SI{69}{\micro\farad} together and so at the table's \SI{68}{\micro\farad}
entry, plus \des{C}{3V3O1} $= \SI{10}{\micro\farad}$ at the pin.
That is \SI{79}{\micro\farad} nominal and \SI{55}{\micro\farad} effective,
clearing the binding requirement by $1.4\times$. Adding the module-local
\SI{22}{\micro\farad} of \cref{sec:esp32-mcu} brings the node to
\SI{101}{\micro\farad} nominal, still inside the \SI{470}{\micro\farad}
ceiling.

\paragraph{Wi-Fi load-step droop.} The maximum load step is
$\Delta I = \SI{0.355}{\ampere}$. At minimum bus-powered input, the inductor
slew rate is
\begin{align*}
  \frac{dI_L}{dt} &= \frac{V_N - V_{OUT}}{L}
    = \frac{8.53-3.3}{\SI{1.5}{\micro\henry}} = \SI{3.49}{\ampere\per\micro\second},
\end{align*}
The inductor reaches the new current in \SI{102}{\nano\second}. This interval
causes less than \SI{1}{\milli\volt} droop. The datasheet does not specify loop
bandwidth. The analysis assumes a \SI{5}{\micro\second} response time
(inferred). Then
\begin{align*}
  \Delta V_{\mathrm{droop}} &\approx
    \frac{\Delta I \cdot t_{\mathrm{resp}}}{C_{\mathrm{out}}} + \Delta I \cdot \mathrm{ESR}
    = \frac{0.355 \times \SI{5}{\micro\second}}{\SI{55}{\micro\farad}}
      + 0.355\times\SI{2.5}{\milli\ohm}
    \approx 32 + 1 \approx \SI{33}{\milli\volt}.
\end{align*}
Power Save Mode works in favor here: it holds the output about
\SI{1}{\percent} high at light load, so the step down into PWM starts from above
the nominal value (\cref{sec:tps63070}).

\paragraph{Accuracy stack against the load's window.} On the adjustable variant
the part specifies its regulation tolerance at the feedback pin
(\cref{sec:tps63070}), so the divider's ratio tolerance and the feedback leakage
of \cref{sec:3v3-fb} add to it at the output. The rail spends
most of its life in Power Save Mode. This mode has the wider $+3/-1\,\%$
feedback tolerance. The $\pm1\,\%$ divider
resistors carry a $\pm1.5\,\%$ ratio error to the output. Leakage contributes
$\pm\SI{12}{\milli\volt}$. Output ripple in Power Save Mode is
\SI{50}{\milli\volt} peak-to-peak at \SI{22}{\micro\farad} effective
(\cref{sec:tps63070}), scaling inversely with capacitance to
$\approx\SI{20}{\milli\volt}$ at the specified \SI{55}{\micro\farad}. Stacking
these with the transient droop above:
\begin{align*}
  V_{\mathrm{rail}}^{\max} &= 0.824\left(1+\frac{121.2}{38.61}\right)
    + 0.012 + 0.020 = \SI{3.44}{\volt}, \\
  V_{\mathrm{rail}}^{\min} &= 0.792\left(1+\frac{118.8}{39.39}\right)
    - 0.012 - 0.020 - 0.033 = \SI{3.12}{\volt},
\end{align*}
against the MCU's \SIrange{3.0}{3.6}{\volt} window (\cref{sec:esp32}):
\SI{116}{\milli\volt} of margin below and \SI{157}{\milli\volt} above. The MCU
absolute maximum equals its recommended maximum. The upper limit includes the
Power Save Mode offset, divider ratio error, leakage, and ripple.

\subsection{Input capacitance}\label{sec:3v3-cin}

The part requires at least \SI{4.7}{\micro\farad} effective at its input pin,
with \SI{10}{\micro\farad} nominal recommended (\cref{sec:tps63070}), and the
input capacitor must be large enough that supply dips shorter than the
undervoltage-lockout circuit's response time do not reach the pin. At the
\SI{12.6}{\volt} node maximum a \SI{25}{\volt}-rated ceramic loses roughly half
its nominal capacitance to bias, so meeting \SI{4.7}{\micro\farad} effective at
that corner requires about \SI{10}{\micro\farad} nominal. Specification: \des{C}{3V3I1},
\des{C}{3V3I2} $= 2\times\SI{10}{\micro\farad}$, \SI{25}{\volt} X7R or X5R, giving
\SI{20}{\micro\farad} nominal and roughly \SI{10}{\micro\farad} effective at the
worst bias, \des{C}{3V3I2} being the pin-local part and \des{C}{3V3I1} the bulk. The
\SI{12.6}{\volt} node maximum sets a floor of \SI{15.8}{\volt} on the rating
under the \SI{20}{\percent} derating used throughout; \SI{25}{\volt} is taken
instead so the bias derating assumed above is achievable.

\des{C}{3V3VAUX} $= \SI{100}{\nano\farad}$ bypasses the VAUX pin, the value the
datasheet requires. VAUX supplies internal loads only and must carry no other
connection. It is a device output, reaching at most \SI{7}{\volt}
(\cref{sec:tps63070}), so its bypass needs $\geq\SI{10}{\volt}$ under the same
derating. The \SI{16}{\volt} grade common in small bodies meets that.

The buck-boost's input current is discontinuous in boost mode but continuous in
buck mode, and the bus-powered case is always the latter. Average input current
at the minimum bus-powered node voltage is
\begin{align*}
  I_{IN} &= \frac{P_{\mathrm{out,max}}}{\eta\,V_N^{\min}}
    = \frac{1.18}{0.85 \times 8.53} = \SI{0.163}{\ampere}
  \quad (\SI{0.228}{\ampere}\ \text{at the \SI{0.5}{\ampere} capability point}),
\end{align*}
drawn from $V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ through \des{Q}{3V3BUS}, the largest of the
logic-rail loads on the bus budget. \des{Q}{3V3BUS} dissipates
\SI{2.8}{\milli\watt} at the sustained-load point and \SI{5.6}{\milli\watt} at
the \SI{0.5}{\ampere} output capability point;
\cref{sec:3v3-diodeor} takes up the choice of switch over diode.

\subsection{Input path and header-powered operation}\label{sec:3v3-diodeor}

Two sources supply the converter input. $V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ supplies it through
\des{Q}{3V3BUS}. The serial-header supply pin (\cref{sec:esp32-uart}) supplies
it through \des{Q}{3V3HDR}. Each branch must block reverse current. Otherwise,
bus power can apply \SI{12.6}{\volt} to the adapter supply pin. Header power
can also energize the bus rail and its bulk capacitance. The higher source
supplies the node.

Each branch uses a MOSFET. The header branch cannot accept a diode drop. A
Schottky diode in the bus branch dissipates \SI{92}{\milli\watt} at
\SI{0.24}{\ampere}. At \SI{0.44}{\ampere} and \SI{0.35}{\volt}, it dissipates
\SI{154}{\milli\watt}. The drop also consumes \SI{29}{\milli\ampere} of the
\SI{500}{\milli\ampere} port budget (\cref{sec:pd-classes}).

\paragraph{Permissible drop in the header branch.} The converter needs
\SI{3.0}{\volt} at its input to start (\cref{sec:tps63070}). The adapter must
supply at least \SI{3.2}{\volt} at the header during download mode. This value
provides a \SI{200}{\milli\volt} drop budget:
\begin{align*}
  V_N^{\mathrm{hdr}} &= \SI{3.2}{\volt} - I_{IN}R_{DSon}, &
  I_{IN} &= \frac{V_{OUT}I_{OUT}}{\eta V_N}
    = \frac{3.3\times0.1}{0.9\times3.15} = \SI{0.12}{\ampere}
\end{align*}
for a \SI{100}{\milli\ampere} download-mode load. A
\SI{150}{\milli\volt} start margin limits the branch drop to
\SI{50}{\milli\volt}. Thus, $R_{DSon} \leq \SI{0.42}{\ohm}$.

A Schottky diode drops \SIrange{0.25}{0.35}{\volt} at this current (inferred).
This drop can reduce the node to \SI{2.9}{\volt} and prevent start-up.
\des{Q}{3V3HDR} is a P-channel MOSFET. Its source connects to the converter
input. Its drain connects to the header supply pin. Its body diode conducts
from the header to the input and blocks current into the adapter.

The \SI{0.42}{\ohm} target reserves \SI{150}{\milli\volt}. The start-up limit
is $\SI{0.2}{\volt}/\SI{0.12}{\ampere}=\SI{1.67}{\ohm}$. The device must meet
the resistance target at approximately \SI{3}{\volt} of gate drive.

\paragraph{Cross-referenced gates.} Each MOSFET must conduct when its source is
higher and block when its source is lower. Each gate references the opposite
supply. \des{Q}{3V3HDR}'s gate connects to $V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ through
\des{R}{3V3HDR}. \des{Q}{3V3BUS}'s gate connects to the header supply through
\des{R}{3V3BUS}. Each source connects to the shared node. Thus, each MOSFET has
\begin{align*}
  V_{GS} &= V_{\mathrm{other}} - V_N, & V_N &\approx \max(V_{\mathrm{BUS}},
    V_{\mathrm{HDR}}),
\end{align*}
The voltage is negative when that branch supplies the node.

\Cref{tab:3v3-oring} gives the gate-source voltages. Except for the last row,
one MOSFET has negative gate drive. The other has $V_{GS}\approx0$. The
datasheet specifies drain leakage at $V_{GS}=0$. The minimum threshold is
\SI{0.65}{\volt} from this condition.

\paragraph{Required separation between the sources.} Passive cross-referenced
ORing requires source separation. \des{Q}{3V3HDR} conducts when
$V_{\mathrm{BUS}}<V_N-|V_{GS(\mathrm{th})}|$. Thus, a higher header supply must
exceed the bus supply by one threshold. In the opposite case,
\des{Q}{3V3BUS} requires $V_N>V_{\mathrm{HDR}}+\SI{2.1}{\volt}$ at the maximum
threshold. A \SI{5}{\volt} contract does not meet these conditions with a
\SI{3.3}{\volt} or \SI{5}{\volt} adapter. Both MOSFETs then operate below
threshold. The higher source supplies the node through a body diode:
$V_N\approx\max(V_{\mathrm{BUS}},V_{\mathrm{HDR}})-V_{SD}$. The converter can
regulate because its running minimum is \SI{2.0}{\volt}.

The complete input current flows through one body diode. The adapter setting
selects the diode:
\begin{description}
  \item[\SI{3.3}{\volt} adapter.] The bus is the higher source.
    \des{Q}{3V3BUS} can conduct through its channel or body diode. At the
    maximum threshold and diode drop, the node is \SI{3.49}{\volt}. The device
    carries \SI{0.40}{\ampere} and dissipates \SI{0.50}{\watt}. This produces
    $T_j=\SI{98}{\celsius}$ at a \SI{40}{\celsius} ambient. The allowance is
    \SI{1.08}{\watt}.
  \item[\SI{5}{\volt} adapter.] The header is the higher source. Current flows
    through \des{Q}{3V3HDR}'s body diode. At the maximum $V_{SD}$, the node is
    \SI{3.80}{\volt}. The device carries
    \begin{align*}
      I_{IN} &= \frac{1.39}{3.80} = \SI{0.366}{\ampere}, &
      P &= I_{IN}V_{SD} = \SI{0.44}{\watt}.
    \end{align*}
\end{description}

The \SI{5}{\volt} adapter case sets the \des{Q}{3V3HDR} package requirement.
Its body diode must carry \SI{0.37}{\ampere} continuously. The package must
dissipate \SI{0.44}{\watt} at a \SI{40}{\celsius} ambient. For a
\SI{125}{\celsius} junction limit, $R_{\mathrm{th}(j-a)}$ must not exceed
\SI{193}{\kelvin\per\watt}. A SOT-23 package meets this requirement. An SC-70
package does not. All other table configurations have more than
\SI{3}{\volt} of source separation.

\paragraph{Gate-voltage ratings.} \des{Q}{3V3BUS}'s gate is near ground when
the node is at \SI{12.6}{\volt}. With \SI{20}{\percent} derating, it requires
$|V_{GS}|\geq\SI{15.8}{\volt}$. \des{Q}{3V3HDR} sees a maximum adapter voltage
of \SI{4.99}{\volt}. It requires $|V_{GS}|\geq\SI{6.2}{\volt}$. A ground
reference would keep a MOSFET on when its branch is the lower source.

\des{R}{3V3HDR} and \des{R}{3V3BUS} are \SI{100}{\kilo\ohm} series gate
resistors. With \des{Q}{3V3BUS}'s \SI{563}{\pico\farad} input capacitance, the
time constant is \SI{56}{\micro\second}. The delay affects turn-off during
hot-plug events. The body diode continues to block reverse current. A
\SI{100}{\nano\ampere} gate leakage current produces a
\SI{10}{\milli\volt} offset. A gate-to-source resistor would form a divider
and reduce gate drive.

\paragraph{Reference-rail discharge.} A reference node is undriven when the
opposite source supplies the input. Leakage current can charge the node. Each
reference node requires a continuous discharge path.

On the bus rail, excess voltage reduces \des{Q}{3V3HDR} gate drive. The
$-\SI{2.5}{\volt}$ resistance specification limits $V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ to
\SI{0.66}{\volt}. The charging current is
\des{Q}{3V3BUS}'s off-state drain leakage, specified as
$I_{DSS}\leq\SI{800}{\nano\ampere}$ at $V_{GS}=0$ and the full
$-\SI{30}{\volt}$ of drain-source voltage (\ds{DMP3099L.pdf}{2}), against the
\SI{3.16}{\volt} operating stress. The junction leakage doubles for each
\SI{10}{\kelvin} increase (inferred). This gives \SI{2.3}{\micro\ampere} at a
\SI{40}{\celsius} ambient:
\begin{align*}
  \des{R}{BUSBLEED} &\leq \frac{\SI{0.66}{\volt}}{\SI{2.3}{\micro\ampere}}
    = \SI{287}{\kilo\ohm}.
\end{align*}
Set \des{R}{BUSBLEED} to \SI{100}{\kilo\ohm}. It holds the rail at
\SI{0.23}{\volt} and gives a $2.9\times$ resistance margin. At
\SI{12.6}{\volt}, it draws \SI{126}{\micro\ampere} and dissipates
\SI{1.59}{\milli\watt}. The calculation does not include other bus loads.

A Schottky diode can leak tens of microamperes at high temperature (inferred).
This leakage can require a bleeder that dissipates \SI{16.4}{\milli\watt}.
The specified MOSFET leakage is approximately 30 times lower.

On the header branch, \des{Q}{3V3HDR} drain leakage charges the undriven supply
pin. The pin must remain below \SI{0.5}{\volt}. This limit prevents current
injection through adapter protection structures.

\des{Q}{3V3HDR}'s off-state drain leakage is required to be
\SI{1}{\micro\ampere} at $T_j = \SI{25}{\celsius}$ and \SI{10}{\micro\ampere} at
\SI{150}{\celsius} (\ds{PMF170XP.pdf}{4}). The off device remains near ambient
temperature. Log-linear interpolation gives \SI{1.3}{\micro\ampere} at
\SI{40}{\celsius} (inferred). Include \des{Q}{3V3BUS}'s
\SI{100}{\nano\ampere} gate leakage through \des{R}{3V3BUS}:
\begin{align*}
  \des{R}{3V3BLEED} &\leq \frac{\SI{0.5}{\volt}}{\SI{1.4}{\micro\ampere}}
    = \SI{357}{\kilo\ohm}.
\end{align*}
Set \des{R}{3V3BLEED} to \SI{100}{\kilo\ohm}. It holds the pin at
\SI{140}{\milli\volt} and gives a $3.6\times$ resistance margin. It draws
\SI{50}{\micro\ampere} from a \SI{5}{\volt} adapter and
\SI{32}{\micro\ampere} from a \SI{3.3}{\volt} adapter.

\des{R}{3V3BLEED} also holds \des{Q}{3V3HDR}'s drain near ground for the
$V_{DS}$ rating calculation. The port controller discharges the bus only after
detach (\cref{sec:pd-bleed}). It does not provide a continuous impedance.
Therefore, populate \des{R}{BUSBLEED}.

\paragraph{Device selection.} \des{Q}{3V3HDR} must meet conduction and body
diode requirements. It must provide no more than \SI{0.42}{\ohm} at
\SI{2.5}{\volt} gate drive and \SI{0.12}{\ampere}. The PMF170XP
(\ds{PMF170XP.pdf}{6})
specifies $R_{DSon} \leq \SI{300}{\milli\ohm}$ at $V_{GS} = -\SI{2.5}{\volt}$
and $T_j = \SI{25}{\celsius}$, which its normalized temperature curve carries to
\SI{321}{\milli\ohm} at the \SI{43}{\celsius} junction that \SI{6}{\milli\watt}
produces in a \SI{40}{\celsius} ambient. It causes a
\SI{38}{\milli\volt} drop. However, its package cannot dissipate the required
\SI{0.44}{\watt}. Select a P-channel MOSFET in SOT-23 or a larger package. It
must also have a \SI{0.37}{\ampere} continuous body-diode rating and a
\SI{6.2}{\volt} gate rating.

\des{Q}{3V3BUS} must meet its resistance specification at
\SI{4.75}{\volt} gate drive. It also requires a \SI{15.8}{\volt} gate rating.
Limiting the node drop to \SI{50}{\milli\volt} at the
\SI{0.41}{\ampere} that band draws requires $R_{DSon}\leq\SI{120}{\milli\ohm}$
hot, so $\leq\SI{110}{\milli\ohm}$ at \SI{25}{\celsius}. The DMP3099L
(\ds{DMP3099L.pdf}{1}), a \SI{30}{\volt} P-channel MOSFET in SOT-23, specifies
$\SI{99}{\milli\ohm}$ maximum at $V_{GS} = -\SI{4.5}{\volt}$ and
$\SI{65}{\milli\ohm}$ at $-\SI{10}{\volt}$, and its normalized temperature curve
gives a factor of 1.08 at a \SI{42}{\celsius} junction. The maximum hot
resistance is \SI{107}{\milli\ohm}. The resistance margin is
\SI{11}{\percent}. Its $V_{DS}$ rating is \SI{30}{\volt}, and its $|V_{GS}|$
rating is \SI{20}{\volt}. Its current rating is \SI{2.9}{\ampere} at
\SI{70}{\celsius}. It dissipates \SI{18}{\milli\watt} against a
\SI{1.08}{\watt} allowance. Its body diode can carry the degraded
configuration's \SI{0.40}{\ampere} at $T_j=\SI{98}{\celsius}$.

\paragraph{Bus-powered operation.} The node sits at
\SIrange{8.53}{12.6}{\volt}. \des{Q}{3V3BUS} has
\SIrange{8.4}{12.5}{\volt} of gate drive. At the \SI{0.5}{\ampere} output
capability point, the input current is \SI{0.228}{\ampere}. The MOSFET drops
\SI{25}{\milli\volt} and dissipates \SI{5.6}{\milli\watt}.
\des{Q}{3V3HDR} is off, and its body diode blocks current
into the adapter.

\paragraph{Header-powered operation, adapter at \SI{3.3}{\volt}.} With
$V_{\mathrm{BUS}}$ absent, \des{Q}{3V3BUS} blocks current into the bus.
\des{Q}{3V3HDR} conducts. The node remains at or above
\SI{3.15}{\volt}. The converter boosts to \SI{3.3}{\volt} with a
\SI{4.5}{\percent} duty cycle.

The adapter must supply \SI{0.12}{\ampere} for the
\SI{100}{\milli\ampere} download-mode load. Some adapters limit their
\SI{3.3}{\volt} outputs to approximately \SI{50}{\milli\ampere} (inferred).
These adapters do not meet the requirement. After start-up, the converter can
operate with an input as low as \SI{2.0}{\volt}.

\paragraph{Header-powered operation, adapter at \SI{5}{\volt}.} The node sits at
\SI{4.99}{\volt}, the converter bucks at a duty cycle of \SI{66}{\percent}, and
the rail remains regulated.

\paragraph{Resulting requirement on the adapter.}\label{sec:3v3-adapter} The
supply pin must present at least \SI{3.2}{\volt} and supply at least
\SI{0.12}{\ampere}. A \SI{3.3}{\volt} or \SI{5}{\volt} setting satisfies the
voltage requirement. \Cref{sec:esp32-uart} specifies the signaling levels.
The ORing circuit permits adapter connection and removal while USB-C power is
present.

Do not attach an adapter while the board uses a \SI{5}{\volt} USB-C contract.
The input then uses body-diode conduction. With a \SI{5}{\volt} adapter,
\des{Q}{3V3HDR} dissipates the diode loss. Use a \SI{9}{\volt} or
\SI{12}{\volt} contract during serial work. Alternatively, disconnect USB-C
power and use only the header supply.

\subsection{Enable, soft start, and dissipation}\label{sec:3v3-house}

EN and PS/SYNC tie to the converter's input node through
$\des{R}{3V3EN}=\SI{10}{\kilo\ohm}$. The pin threshold is
\SIrange{0.77}{0.83}{\volt} (\cref{sec:tps63070}). This tolerance does not
permit a divider to separate the \SI{3.0}{\volt} start limit from the
\SI{3.15}{\volt} input minimum. The internal undervoltage lockout controls
start-up. \des{R}{3V3EN} permits an external circuit to pull both pins low.
The available states are off and Power Save Mode. Forced-PWM operation is not
available. Tie VSEL low to use the \des{R}{3V3D1}/\des{R}{3V3D2} divider. Tie
the unused FB2 pin to ground as permitted by the datasheet
(\ds{tps63070.pdf}{4}).

PG is an open-drain output. \des{R}{3V3PG}=\SI{100}{\kilo\ohm} pulls it up to
the rail.

PG drives the MCU enable input (\cref{sec:esp32-reset}). PG releases above
\SIrange{94.5}{98.5}{\percent} of the output and reasserts below
\SIrange{90}{94.5}{\percent} (\cref{sec:tps63070}), which at the programmed
\SI{3.262}{\volt} is \SIrange{3.08}{3.21}{\volt} rising and
\SIrange{2.94}{3.08}{\volt} falling. The rising band sits above the MCU's
\SI{3.0}{\volt} supply minimum (\cref{sec:esp32}). Thus, PG releases the MCU
only after the rail enters its specified range. While asserted, PG sinks
\SI{33}{\micro\ampere}. Its rating is \SI{1}{\milli\ampere}.

Internal soft start is \SI{400}{\micro\second} in buck operation and
\SI{850}{\micro\second} in boost operation. The output has
\SI{71}{\micro\farad} of effective capacitance. The maximum charging current is
\begin{align*}
  I_{\mathrm{chg}} &= C_{\mathrm{out}}\frac{dV}{dt}
    = \SI{71}{\micro\farad}\times\frac{\SI{3.27}{\volt}}{\SI{400}{\micro\second}}
    = \SI{0.58}{\ampere}
\end{align*}
in buck operation. The start-up current limit is \SI{1}{\ampere}. If the load
exceeds the available current, the current limit extends the ramp.

Converter dissipation at the conservative $\eta=0.85$:
\begin{align*}
  P_{\mathrm{diss}} &= P_{\mathrm{out,max}}\left(\frac{1}{\eta}-1\right)
    = 1.18 \times 0.176 = \SI{0.21}{\watt}, &
  \Delta T &= \theta_{JA}P_{\mathrm{diss}} = 63 \times 0.21 \approx \SI{13}{\kelvin},
\end{align*}
rising to \SI{0.29}{\watt} and $\Delta T\approx\SI{18}{\kelvin}$ at
\SI{0.5}{\ampere}. At a \SI{40}{\celsius} ambient, $T_J$ is at most
\SI{58}{\celsius}. The junction limit is \SI{125}{\celsius}.

\subsection{Summary}\label{sec:3v3-summary}

The analysis uses the following inferred values. MOSFET leakage doubles for
each \SI{10}{\kelvin} temperature increase. Output ceramic capacitors retain
at least \SI{70}{\percent} of nominal capacitance at \SI{3.3}{\volt}. Input
ceramic capacitors retain \SI{50}{\percent} at \SI{12.6}{\volt}. Converter
efficiency is \SI{85}{\percent}. The control loop responds within
\SI{5}{\micro\second}. Download-mode MCU current is limited to
\SI{100}{\milli\ampere}. The adapter must supply at least \SI{3.2}{\volt} at
the header, and the header switch resistance must not exceed \SI{0.42}{\ohm}.
