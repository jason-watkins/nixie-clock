\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This document derives the key electrical characteristics of the Nixie
Clock board from the datasheets of its major components and from
first-principles circuit analysis. The analysis stays at the
schematic level: values follow from component ratings and circuit
topology rather than from a specific PCB footprint, except where a
part is inseparable from one particular package. Every numeric claim
about a named, design-driving component traces to a page in a
datasheet under \texttt{docs/datasheets/}; anything not covered by a
datasheet there is sized to standard engineering practice instead. The
document proceeds subsystem by subsystem, working backward from the
load: the Nixie tubes and their binary-coded-decimal (BCD)
decoder/drivers set the
requirements for the 170~V anode supply and for the 5~V rail that
powers those same drivers, the ESP32 MCU sets the requirements for the
3.3~V rail, and all three converters together set the requirements
for the USB-PD front end that supplies them.
\end{abstract}
